# Citation Notes

## Evidence Use

- Cite data sources where datasets are first introduced.
- Cite methods literature where the accessibility metric or spatial model is justified.
- Cite policy documents only for policy context, not as proof of empirical findings.

## Citation Hygiene

- Keep citation style consistent within the document.
- Do not cite a source for a claim it does not directly support.
- Preserve publication year and institutional author names accurately.
- Mark missing bibliographic details instead of inventing them.

## Paraphrasing

- Paraphrase findings in project language.
- Use short direct quotations only when wording is analytically important.
- Keep translated source concepts faithful to the original meaning.
